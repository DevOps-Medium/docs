# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 13, 2026_

This policy explains what personal data DevOps Medium ("we", "us") collects when you use [devopsmedium.com](https://devopsmedium.com), why we collect it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email <hello@devopsmedium.com>.

## Who We Are

DevOps Medium is run by Nabeel Hassan, an individual based in Pakistan, who decides how and why your personal data is used (the "controller" under data protection laws such as the GDPR). You can reach us at <hello@devopsmedium.com> or through the [contact form](/contactus).

## Summary

- You can read every tutorial, lab manual and article, and take every course, without an account.
- If you create a free account, we keep your email address, course progress and quiz results so they follow you across devices. You can download or delete them at any time from [your account page](/account).
- When you contact us, we use what you send only to reply to you.
- We measure how the site is used with Cloudflare Web Analytics (no cookies) and Google Analytics (cookies you can turn off).
- We don't sell your data.
- We show ads from Google AdSense. Outside the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, ads are personalised and analytics cookies are on unless you choose **Necessary Only**. In those regions, Google asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie Settings** in the site footer.

## Data We Collect

### When You Contact Us

The [contact form](/contactus) asks for your name, email address, an optional subject and your message. We also record your approximate country, which our hosting provider derives from your IP address. We deliver it to our own inbox through Google's Gmail so we can reply. We keep a conversation while it's open and for as long as we may need it afterwards: to follow up, to answer related questions you send later, or to deal with a dispute about it. We review our inbox at least once a year and delete conversations we no longer need. You can ask us to delete yours at any time, and we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

### When You Browse

Our hosting and content delivery provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data needed to deliver the site and keep it secure: your IP address, browser user agent, the pages you request and the time of each request. We use Cloudflare Turnstile to protect the contact form and the sign-in form from spam. Turnstile checks your browser without tracking you across sites.

### Analytics

We use two tools to understand which pages help and how people find them:

- **Cloudflare Web Analytics** counts page views, referrers, countries, browsers and page speed. It doesn't use cookies or identify you.
- **Google Analytics** measures visits, pages viewed, approximate location (from your IP address, which Google doesn't store), device and browser, and how you arrived. It uses cookies when you allow analytics. When you don't, it sends Google cookieless measurements without identifiers, which Google uses to estimate traffic.

We see only aggregated reports. We don't combine analytics with the other data in this policy.

### When You Search

Search queries are sent to our database provider, Supabase, to find matching pages. We don't link queries to you or keep a search history.

### Course Progress in Your Browser

When you mark a lesson complete or check a quiz, your browser remembers it in its local storage (`dm:progress`, `dm:quiz`), so the course page can show your progress. Without an account, this stays on your device and is never sent to us.

### Learner Accounts

You can create a free account to keep your course progress and quiz results on every device. Signing in for the first time, with an email link or your GitHub account, creates it. For your account we store:

- **Your email address**, to sign you in and to contact you about your account if we need to.
- **With GitHub sign-in**, the details GitHub shares with us: your GitHub user ID, username, name and avatar address.
- **A display name**, taken from your GitHub name or the start of your email address. You can change it on your account page. Nobody else sees it.
- **Your learning record**: which lessons you completed and when, and each quiz attempt (your answers and score). Progress saved in your browser before you signed in is added to your account.
- **Sign-in records** kept by our sign-in provider, Supabase, for security: when you signed in, your IP address and browser.

Your account is private: it isn't shown to other people and we don't use it for advertising. We keep it until you delete it. On [your account page](/account) you can download your data as a file and delete your account. Deleting it removes your account, profile, progress and quiz results from our database straight away. Copies in our database provider's backups are overwritten within a few weeks. Progress saved in your browser stays until you clear your browser data.

### Staff Accounts

Authors and editors are invited and sign in the same way. For them we also store their role, and the docs they write and change are credited to them on the site, with their name, profile page and avatar.

### Your Region

To decide whether to show our cookie notice or Google's consent message, your browser asks our server whether you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland. The server works this out from your IP address and answers only yes or no; nothing is stored.

## Cookies

We use a small number of cookies and similar browser storage. Necessary items are always on. Google Analytics sets cookies to measure visits, and Google AdSense sets advertising cookies to personalise and measure ads; you can turn both off.

| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| `dm_consent`                             | DevOps Medium            | Remembers your cookie choice                                                   | Necessary                                       | 6 months                       |
| `theme` (local storage)                  | DevOps Medium            | Remembers light or dark theme                                                  | Necessary                                       | Until you clear it             |
| `dm:tabs` (local storage)                | DevOps Medium            | Remembers code tab choices, e.g. Helm or kubectl                               | Necessary                                       | Until you clear it             |

To change your choice, use **Cookie Settings** in the footer of any page. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, it opens Google's consent settings. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you do, some preferences won't be remembered.

## Advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense to keep our content free.

- **In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland**, Google's consent message (a consent tool certified by Google) asks before any advertising or analytics cookies are used. Until you choose, Google receives only signals that consent hasn't been given.
- **Elsewhere**, ads are personalised by default. If you choose **Necessary Only** or turn off personalised advertising in **Cookie Settings**, we ask Google for non-personalised ads and pass your choice to Google through Google Consent Mode.

Non-personalised ads can still use cookies for frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's [Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- Learn [how Google uses data when you use our partners' sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Third-Party Content

- **YouTube videos** use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (`youtube-nocookie.com`) and load only when you press play.
- **Author photos** are loaded from GitHub, which receives your IP address when your browser fetches the image.
- **Links to other sites**, such as GitHub repositories, are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

## How We Use Your Data and Our Legal Bases

- **To reply to you** when you contact us: our legitimate interest in answering your request, or steps you asked us to take.
- **To provide your learner account**, including saving your progress and quiz results: our agreement with you (the [Terms of Use](/terms)) when you create an account.
- **To run and secure the site**, including sign-in records, staff accounts and cookieless analytics: our legitimate interest in a working, safe website that we can improve.
- **To show ads and use analytics cookies**: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Elsewhere, our legitimate interest in funding free content and improving it, and you can opt out at any time.

## Sharing and International Transfers

We share data only with the service providers that run the site for us: Cloudflare (hosting, security, spam protection, analytics), Supabase (database, sign-in and sign-in emails), Google (Gmail for contact form messages, Analytics, advertising) and GitHub (sign-in with GitHub, when you choose it). We're based in Pakistan, and these providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where required, they protect it with appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent at any time. If you have an account, you can download and delete your data yourself on [your account page](/account). For anything else, email <hello@devopsmedium.com>. We'll reply within one month, and may ask you to confirm your identity first. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

DevOps Medium is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal data. You must be at least 13, or older where your country's law requires it, to create an account. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We'll update this page when our practices change, and revise the date at the top. Significant changes will be highlighted on the site.
